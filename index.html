Personal Mail Reader

A private, single-user tool that lets its owner ask an AI assistant to search and read the owner's own Gmail, with read-only access.

What it does

  • It runs only on its owner's computer, as a local extension (an MCP server) for Claude Code (Anthropic) and ChatGPT Desktop/Codex (OpenAI).
  • When the owner asks, it searches and reads messages in Gmail accounts the owner has linked, and saves an attachment to the owner's computer if the owner requests it.
  • It asks Google for one permission: read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change email, labels or settings.

Who it is for

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public and there is nothing to sign up for. If you are not the owner, please don't authorize it.
